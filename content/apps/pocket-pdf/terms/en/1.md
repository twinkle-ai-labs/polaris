## 3. What the Service does

The Operator provides the following functions.

- Merging several PDF documents, and splitting one to extract only the pages you want
- Converting photos and images into a PDF
- On-device OCR that reads text from scanned documents and images, and creates a searchable PDF
- Document analysis using an on-device model — reading the text, summarizing it, and finding dates, amounts and contacts
- Creating an unlocked copy of a password-protected PDF

All document processing happens on your device. Your documents and any password you enter are never sent to the Operator's servers.

Document analysis is available **only on devices that support the on-device model**. Whether a device supports it is decided by the device and its operating system and cannot be guaranteed by the Operator — on devices that do not, the feature is not shown at all.
